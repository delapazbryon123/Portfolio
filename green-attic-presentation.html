THE DISCOVERY PROCESS

Identifying Operational Bottlenecks

1. Strategic Analysis: In evaluating the Enterprise Architect position, I mapped the standard customer journey and operational workflows of high-volume insulation contractors against Green Attic's existing tech stack and market positioning.

2. Identifying the Gaps: This analysis revealed common industry friction points that often limit scaling: manual data handoffs between sales and Field Operations (CRM to ClickUp), inconsistent "field-to-feed" marketing content generation, and the massive resource costs associated with traditional commercial B2B outreach.

3. The Proposed Architecture: What follows is a blueprint for a custom, decoupled n8n architecture designed specifically to eliminate manual data entry, connect siloed systems via APIs, and fully automate operations natively without ballooning SaaS costs.

THE CHALLENGE

General Industry Friction Points

1. CRM-to-ClickUp Handoff Friction: Transitioning from a won deal in the CRM to an actionable, fully-scoped project in ClickUp often requires extensive manual data entry. This creates data silos and increases the likelihood of miscommunication between sales and field operations.

2. Stagnant Local SEO: Maintaining a hyper-local blog presence to capture Generative AI search traffic requires immense manual writing time, making it difficult to compete organically against national franchises with massive marketing budgets.

3. The Content Bottleneck: Field installers take excellent job-site photos, but transferring those images to marketing, writing unique localized captions, and scheduling them across multiple networks is tedious and frequently neglected.

4. The "Project Gap" Anxiety: During multi-day projects, homeowners can experience anxiety if they aren't proactively updated between phases. Manual communication often drops off when field crews are busy, leading to decreased customer satisfaction.

5. Expensive B2B Lead Gen: Commercial insulation is highly lucrative, but hiring dedicated outbound SDRs is expensive and traditional manual cold-calling yields diminishing returns.
