CASE STUDY: GREEN ATTIC

Omnichannel Automation

Evolving Green Attic into a tech-enabled powerhouse through decoupled, n8n-driven intelligent workflows and B2B Lead Generation.

4.8 Google Rating Managed
B2B Commercial Lead Gen
∞ Scalability with n8n
THE CHALLENGE

Admin Bloat & Invisible Value

Pain Point 1: Social Media Bottlenecks. Taking amazing post-job photos and 5-star reviews and formatting them for 4 different platforms is too time-consuming for a busy contractor.


Pain Point 2: Invisible ROI. Insulation relies on long-term value. Customers hesitate because they can't easily calculate their upfront costs vs. long-term savings.


Pain Point 3: The Project Gap. During multi-day projects (removal vs. installation), poor communication can lead to homeowner anxiety.

THE SOLUTION

Automation-Driven Experience

🤖

AI B2B Lead Generation

Unlock commercial warehouse jobs. A custom Python GUI workflow automatically scrapes LinkedIn for Chicago Property Managers and sends personalized outreach emails.

✉️

Proactive SMS Updates

Eliminates the "Project Gap" anxiety. Automated Twilio SMS updates are sent to the customer as soon as the CRM status changes on the field iPad (e.g., "Removal Complete").

⭐

Omnichannel Proof Engine

Maximizes organic growth. When a new 5-star review drops, an n8n webhook triggers an AI to generate a beautiful branded graphic and schedules it to Facebook and Instagram automatically.

ARCHITECTURE

Decoupled Workflow System

Treating the website purely as a UI and utilizing n8n for the heavy lifting (Data + Logic) ensures infinite scalability and a premium customer experience.

UI LAYER: WordPress

Lead Capture & Front-end Interaction

WORKFLOW LAYER: n8n Brain

Twilio SMS, Image Generation, B2B Agent Logic

DATA LAYER: CRM / AirTable

Customer Single Source of Truth
