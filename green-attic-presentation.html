CASE STUDY: GREEN ATTIC

Deep Research & Decoupled UX Strategy

Solving customer anxiety and operational friction for a $M+ insulation contractor through intelligent workflow design and premium brand repositioning.

5.0 Facebook Rating Managed
2026 Quality Business Award
30% Avg. Energy Bill Reduction
THE CHALLENGE

The "Invisible Value" & Comms Gap

Pain Point 1: The Project Gap. Research identified a critical communication blackout during the 24-48 hour window between insulation removal and re-installation.


Pain Point 2: Value Perception. Insulation is "invisible." Customers often struggle to justify high upfront costs for results they can't see but only feel over months.

THE SOLUTION

Automation-First Experience

✉️

Phase-Transition SMS

Automated real-time updates triggered by field completion, eliminating customer "downtime" anxiety.

🧮

Interactive ROI Tool

A dynamic Lead Magnet that visualizes 10-year energy savings, turning "invisible work" into "tangible investment."

📑

One-Click Rebate Filing

Automated PDF generation for utility rebates (ComEd/Peoples Gas) using data scraped from work-orders.

ARCHITECTURE

Decoupled Workflow System

My strategic approach separates the UI from the Business Logic for maximum reliability and scalability.

UI LAYER: WordPress

Lead Capture & Front-end Content

WORKFLOW LAYER: n8n / AI Agent

Brain: SMS Logic, PDF Gen, ROI Calculations

DATA LAYER: CRM / Database

Customer Single Source of Truth
