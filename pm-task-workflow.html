How It Works — End-to-End Flow

Stage 1: Transcription

After a Zoom/Google Meet call ends, the recording is automatically pushed to an AI transcription service (e.g., Whisper) to generate a high-accuracy text transcript.

Stage 2: Semantic Analysis

OpenAI receives the transcript, summarizes the meeting, and extracts explicit action items, identifying who is responsible and the agreed-upon deadlines.

Stage 3: Data Formatting

n8n formats the AI's output into structured API payloads matching ClickUp's required format for task creation, including custom fields and space/folder routing.

Stage 4: Task Creation & Notification

Tasks are deployed into ClickUp workspaces. Simultaneously, a summary email or Slack message is sent to the team detailing the newly created action items.
