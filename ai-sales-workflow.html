How It Works — End-to-End Flow

Stage 1: Lead Capture

A lead is captured via web form, social media ad, or direct outreach, immediately triggering the automation sequence in n8n.

Stage 2: Voice AI Engagement

The Voice AI calls the lead to qualify their intent, understand their needs, and determine if they are a good fit for the business.

Stage 3: Information Processing

The call transcript is routed to OpenAI, where GPT analyzes the sentiment, extracts key data points, and categorizes the lead's pain points.

Stage 4: Content Generation & CRM Sync

OpenAI generates personalized follow-up content based on the call. The CRM is seamlessly updated with tags, lead status, notes, and the custom follow-up materials.
